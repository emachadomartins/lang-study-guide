## Exercises

1. **First project** — Create the `taskflow` project, run it and change the welcome message/screen to "TaskFlow".
2. **Explore the structure** — For each generated file, write one line about what it is for (keep the notes in your project's README).
3. Build TaskFlow's first "hello" in your category:
   - 🖥️ **Backend:** `GET /health` returning `{"status": "ok"}`.
   - 🎨 **Frontend** / 📱 **Mobile:** a home screen titled "TaskFlow" with a hard-coded list of 3 tasks.
   - 📊 **Data/ML:** generate/load a dataset of 1,000 fake tasks (with every field of the domain) and show the first 5 rows.
