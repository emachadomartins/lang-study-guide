## Exercises

1. **Word frequency** — Given a text, count how often each word appears (ignoring case and punctuation) and show the 10 most frequent.
2. **Remove duplicates** — Remove duplicates from a list **while preserving** the original order.
3. **Two Sum** — Given a list of integers and a target, find the indices of two numbers that add up to the target, in O(n).
4. **Grouping** — Given a list of people `(name, city)`, group the names by city.
5. **Stack: balanced brackets** — Check whether a string containing `()[]{}` is balanced.
6. **Matrix** — Transpose an NxM matrix and multiply two matrices.
7. **Address book** — In-memory CRUD of contacts (name, phone, email) with search by name.
